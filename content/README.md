# content

Typed, versioned content constants — Compare slides, Why Us values, Sectors,
Contact enquiry types, About copy, and plate artwork.

Per DEC-011, Firestore backs the Trusted By section **only**. Every other piece
of site copy lives here in the codebase and changes through a normal code
review, not through an admin panel.

Copy in this folder must be traceable to `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`. Do not invent
business facts, statistics, partner names, or legal wording to fill a gap — open
an entry in `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` §25 instead.
